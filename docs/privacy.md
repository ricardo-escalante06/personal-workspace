# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 26, 2026_

Personal Workspace is a personal productivity app for Mac and iPhone. This page explains what it does with your data.

## What the app stores

- **Your tasks, projects and notes.** These are saved on your device and synced to a private database (hosted by Supabase) so they appear on your other devices. Only your signed-in account can read them.
- **Google Calendar data.** If you connect a Google account, the app reads your calendars and events so it can show them, and changes events only when you ask it to (for example, moving, editing, deleting or replying to an event). Events are cached on your device. They are not copied into the app's sync database; only your own settings about them are synced, such as which calendar or event belongs to which class or project.
- **Sign-in tokens** for Google are kept in your device's Keychain.

## What the app does not do

- It does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
- It does not show ads or use your data for advertising.
- It does not use Google data to train AI or machine learning models.
- No person reads your data. It is used only to provide the app's features to you.

## Google API Services

Personal Workspace's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removing your data

- Disconnect a Google account in the app's Settings, or remove the app's access at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
- Deleting the app removes the data stored on that device.
- To have your synced data deleted, open an issue on the project's [GitHub page](https://github.com/ricardo-escalante06/personal-workspace/issues).

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the project's [GitHub page](https://github.com/ricardo-escalante06/personal-workspace/issues).
